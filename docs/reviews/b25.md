# QA review: batch b25 (q0420-q0438)

I recomputed every worked example and quiz answer with `node`. That covered the bridge conduction and dead-time losses, PWM ripple, the braking transients (τm, plug time, energies, angles), the asymmetric FET split, the bootstrap sizing and droop, shoot-through energy, the solenoid flyback decay, the gate RLC damping, the optocoupler CTR, pull-up and emitter-follower numbers, barrier impedance, and the PFET level-shifter dissipation and switching loss.

I screenshotted every figure and every sim in the running app and read each one, including the q0432, q0433 and q0436 mermaid diagrams and the q0421 and q0428 timing diagrams. I ran the sim models (h-bridge-lab, bootstrap-driver, gate-drive, opto-isolator, flyback-protection, ringing-lc) with each page's params, so every caption describes what the sim actually shows. All sim params match catalog.json. The validator reports 0 errors.

**Most serious:**
- **q0424:** the short-circuit brake schematic drew the braking current in the same direction as forward drive. The braking current reverses.
- **q0428:** the sim caption told the reader to close "Q1 and Q3 (same leg)". In the sim that is a high-side brake, not shoot-through, because the sim numbers its legs Q1/Q2 and Q3/Q4.
- **q0423:** the sim caption described comparing "half-bridge mode with H-bridge mode" in a sim fixed to half-bridge mode.
- **q0424:** the dc-motor-lab caption claimed the sim could show the motor stopping. It only simulates start-up.
- **q0435:** the buffer gate current ignored the share of photocurrent taken by the 6.8 kΩ pulldown. The page also never said that the phototransistor's microsecond delays dominate end-to-end timing.
