# QA review: batch b26 (q0439-q0453)

I recomputed every worked example and quiz answer with `node`. That covered shunt loss and resolution, CT and Rogowski outputs, Hall offset equivalents, coulomb-count drift, short-circuit di/dt, the CMRR from matching and from the bus, the value windows, the Rds(on) temperature model, the gain selections and the RSS budgets.

I screenshotted every figure and sim in the running app and read each one. I ran the current-sense model with each page's params (current-sense, soc-estimator, dmm-lab, mosfet-lab, diff-amp-cmrr) and rewrote every caption that claimed something the sim doesn't show. All sim params match catalog.json. The validator reports 0 errors.

**Most serious:**
- **q0441:** the Hall-effect SVG put both V_H leads on the same edge of the plate. That measures the longitudinal voltage, not the Hall voltage.
- **q0439:** the no-amplifier resolution was wrong ("16 mA per count"; it is 81 mA).
- **Current-sense sim captions:** in q0439, q0446, q0450 and q0453 (all with I_min = 10 mA at 10 mΩ, G = 50), the captions never mentioned that the sim's 150 µV offset and 5 mV output floor put I_min inside a 25 mA dead zone that reads zero. Several captions also promised a common-mode warning that does not appear at the sim's default 12 V bus.
