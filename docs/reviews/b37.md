# QA review: batch b37 (q0652-q0670)

I recomputed every quiz answer (110 items) and every worked number with `node`. All quiz answer indices, values and tolerances were already correct. The numbers I checked:

- **Yield and wafers:** Poisson yield, dies per wafer, known-good-die compounding.
- **Bond wires:** HBM bandwidth, TSV R, R = t/kA, bond-wire R, L and skin depth, adiabatic fusing (I²t ≈ 13.5 A²·ms re-derived with temperature-dependent ρ), coupled parallel-wire inductance.
- **Flip chip:** bump L/R and CTE strain.
- **Ground bounce:** di/dt model, PDN f0.
- **Fab constants:**
  - Deal-Grove: A = 0.165 µm, B = 0.0117 µm²/h and τ = 0.37 h are the textbook 1000 °C dry-O₂ values. They give 68.6 nm at 1 h and 158 nm at 4 h.
  - Rayleigh: the k1/DOF table rows are all correct. k1 = 0.28 at an 80 nm pitch.
  - EUV dose: 30 mJ/cm² gives about 20 photons/nm² at 13.5 nm and about 290 at 193 nm, so about 2000 photons in a 10×10 nm feature (≈2% shot noise).
- **Etch and ALD:** etch undercut and mask thickness, KOH 54.74°, ALD cycles and EOT.
- **Signals:** SQNR table, jitter SNR, sinc and Fourier-series coefficients, Gibbs 1.18, raw-peak error 1−cos(π/N), bandpass-sampling windows, Butterworth order estimates, noise folding (√200 = 14.1).
