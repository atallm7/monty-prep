# QA review: batch b10 (q0156-q0172)

I recomputed every quiz answer, table and worked example with `node`, including a numerical simulation of the q0160 bridge rectifier. I rendered all schematics and read the PNGs. I checked sim params against catalog.json and screenshotted the mermaid and bode figures in the running app. The validator reports 0 errors.

- **q0156**: Fixed four things:
  - The "12 V in at 0.3 A" buck example was muddled. It now says 0.275 A ideal, about 0.30 A at 92%, versus 1 A for an LDO.
  - The Li-ion-to-5 V follow-up said an LDO "fails below 5.2 V". It now says an LDO cannot work at all because the cell never exceeds 5 V.
  - The interpretations said q0157/q0167 have "the same wording"; they are siblings with different wording.
  - Removed a vague TI reference.
  - Also changed the mermaid tree from TD to LR. The text was unreadably small.
- **q0157**: Reworded the ripple example ("pick L for 30% ripple" produced 50%), so it is now stated as a given 4.7 µH design. Numbers verified.
- **q0158**: Corrected the light-load plot caption. A 50 µA-Iq LDO is worse than the 92% buck across the whole range, not just below 300 µA (3.3/3.6 = 91.7% < 92%).
- **q0159**: The DC bus after rectification was given as "about 300 V". It is now about 170 V on 120 V mains and 325 V on 230 V, consistent with the rest of the page.
- **q0160**: Cross-links for diode basics pointed to q0197 (the buck freewheel diode). They now point to q0072 and q0078. The simulation claims (14.7 V peak, 1.3 V ripple, 2.6 A diode peaks) reproduce in my own simulation.
- **q0161**: Fixed two things:
  - Clarified "boost needs 4× more capacitance". It is 4× the ripple at the same C.
  - Fixed the muddled AA/NiMH example (2-cell range vs the single 1.2 V cell calculation).
- **q0162**: Fixed two things:
  - The calorimetry uncertainty is now about 7 W combined, consistent with the quiz.
  - The R_DS(on) tempco was "about 0.4%/°C" and is now "roughly 0.5%/°C".
  - Also changed the measurement-setup mermaid to TD for legibility.
- **q0163**: OK.
- **q0164**: OK. The Arrhenius factors (2.08, 7.95, 9.76) and thermal numbers are verified.
- **q0165**: OK.
- **q0166**: OK. The power budget, cascade comparison (87.1% vs 91.0%), inductors, input RMS currents and on-times are verified.
- **q0167**: Fixed two things:
  - The transformer turns ratio "ignoring drops ≈ 0.44" was wrong (that value includes drops). It now reads 0.42 ignoring drops and 0.44 with drops.
  - The example made +5 V analog with "an LDO from the 5 V rail", which is impossible with no headroom. It now uses an LDO from 12 V for +5 V (0.14 W), and an inverting converter to about −6 V plus a negative LDO for −5 V.
- **q0168**: Fixed the bode caption. The damped LC (R = 0.3 Ω, Q ≈ 1) peaks about 1.5 dB, not "under 1 dB". Bode screenshot checked.
- **q0169**: OK. The chain efficiencies and losses are verified.
- **q0170**: "At 1 V and 300 A the path would dissipate kilowatts" was wrong. It is now 450 W (300² × 5 mΩ).
- **q0171**: The page said q0706 was a "firmware-oriented" section. It is the power-integrity section; fixed in two places. The back-power example (2.26 V, 6.8 mA) is verified.
- **q0172**: Fixed the same q0706 section label. The RC delay numbers (4.52 ms, 3.2–6.1 ms spread) are verified.

Remaining concerns:
