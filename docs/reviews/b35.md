## Sims on q0620 and q0621 (specifically asked)

- **q0620 (tline-lab):** the sim was the default open-ended unterminated line (`term: none, rl: open`), and the caption only loosely related it to test-point stubs. I kept tline-lab but configured it as an exact model of a test-point stub: `z0 50, rs 25, rl open, td 50 ps, tr 100 ps`.
  - Seen from the tap, the two halves of the trunk are 25 Ω, so the "V at source end" trace is the voltage at the tap, which is what reaches the receiver.
  - I screenshotted it. The tap lags by about a third of the swing, then overshoots 11% at 2td.
  - The caption now tells the learner to shrink td (stub length) and raise tr (slow bus) and to watch the distortion vanish.
- **q0621 (microstrip-calc):** the old caption ("a pad lowers local impedance") was a weak fit. I kept the sim because its L′/C′-per-mm readout directly sizes a stub's parasitics, and set `type microstrip, w 0.3, h 0.2`. I checked the values against the sim's own model:
  - 0.11 pF/mm, 0.32 nH/mm and 5.8 ps/mm, so a 5 mm stub adds about 0.5 pF and about 30 ps.
  - At w = 1.0 mm (pad width), Z0 falls from 55 Ω to 26 Ω.
  - The caption now teaches the "pad on the trace, no stub" guideline with these numbers.

## Most serious errors fixed

- **q0626, freeze-spray physics backwards:** three places said freeze spray or alcohol "evaporates/defrosts last" over the hot spot. It defrosts or evaporates first there. Fixed in the technical, terms and deep sections. Thermal camera sensitivity "a few mK" is now "a few tens of mK".
- **q0617, sim param bug:** the e-series sim had `tol: 1`, which means 100% tolerance; the sim silently fell back to its 5% default. It is now `value 93000, series E24, tol 0.01`, tied to the page's divider example. R₃ ≈ 93 kΩ gives 3.6 V; E24 91 kΩ gives 3.61 V, E96 93.1 kΩ gives 3.60 V, and E12 offers 82 k (3.64 V) or 100 k (3.58 V).
- **q0627, wrong caption on the measurement-bw sim:** the caption promised overshoot/ringing appearing or vanishing, but the sim models a Gaussian front end with no overshoot. Rewritten to what the sim shows: 1 ns edge reads about 2 ns at 200 MHz, about 6% error at 1 GHz, and the sample-rate and phase effect.
- **q0627, probe-impedance plot caption:** said the 10× probe is capacitive "above a few hundred kHz". The corner is about 1.6 kHz. I widened the plot to 100 Hz, added the active-probe curve, and corrected the claim that active probes stay above 1 kΩ "to hundreds of MHz" to "up to roughly 150 MHz".
- **q0628, Kelvin explanation wrong:** it attributed a 50% error to "5 mΩ contact resistance" of the voltmeter probes. A voltmeter draws no current, so its contact resistance doesn't matter. The real issue is sensing outside the solder joints and trace in the current path; rewritten with the correct example (37.5 mV read instead of 25 mV).
- **q0621/q0622, stub inductance:** both pages gave "about 1 nH/mm" for a trace stub over a plane. A 0.3 mm trace over 0.2 mm FR-4 is about 0.3 nH/mm; 1 nH/mm applies to wires far from their return. Corrected with the calculator values.
- **q0622, inconsistent fixture cost:** the technical section said USD 1-10 per pin "including fixture build", while the deep section's example works out to USD 150 per probe. Both now distinguish marginal cost from average cost per probe. q0625 was aligned the same way.
