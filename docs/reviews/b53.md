## Remaining concerns

- The illustrative numeric models differ between pages and the sim. The pages use a 20 nF, 30 to 100 W chip with θ_JA of 0.5 to 0.6 K/W; the sim uses a 5 W chip with θ_JA of 10 K/W. The pages' leakage plots assume doubling every 15 °C, while the sim model is closer to every 24 °C. Each is labeled illustrative and the captions now say the sim chip differs, but a reader may notice.
- The pvt-power sim model is aggressive: FF at a 25 °C ambient with θ_JA = 10 °C/W has no steady state (runaway). That is physically possible with a poor heatsink but harsh as a default; it is the sim's design, not page content, and is left as is.
- In the regulator-compare sim, the buck loses to the LDO again above about 2.4 A at 1.0 to 0.8 V, because of its conduction loss with the default 80 mΩ. That is plausible, but worth knowing if a learner pushes the load slider.
