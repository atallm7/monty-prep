# QA review: batch b46 (q0808-q0822)

I reviewed 15 pages: 12 on ADCs (q0808-q0819) and 3 on DACs (q0820-q0822). I recomputed every quiz answer (88 items) and every worked number with `node`. All quiz answer indices, numeric answers and tolerances were correct. The errors I found were in the body text: wrong figures, inconsistent numbers and some loose physics.

**Numbers checked.**

- **ADC basics:** LSB, code and bin numbers (1.234 V gives code 382 at 10 bits; 3000 gives 2.417 V at 12 bits; the offset-reference, divider, two's-complement and internal-reference cases); the SNR derivation (6.02N+1.76) and the ENOB and noise-free bits examples; the error budget (worst case 11.1 LSB, RSS 5.68 LSB, 3.4 LSB after calibration).
- **Flash and SAR:** the flash threshold and thermometer table and the comparator-count and power scaling; the skew-limited input frequency (123 MHz); the full 10-step SAR trial table, which I re-ran as an algorithm; kT/C, settling ((N+1)·ln 2 time constants) and conversion-rate figures.
- **Sample-and-hold:** aperture times (77.7 ns at 1 kHz); droop and hold-time table; the C_H trade table (noise added in quadrature, droop, pedestal); bootstrapped R_on; jitter-limited SNR; bucket-capacitor dip and sizing; the throughput-versus-sampling-cycles numbers.
- **Front end:** RC attenuation at 50 and 90 kHz, divider and Thevenin values, gain-66 and gain-330 scaling, thermistor sensitivity (36.7 mV/K).
- **DAC:** the R-2R Thevenin derivation; PWM two-stage filter values (x = 18, τ = 46 µs, settling 16.8τ) and the 10-bit and 12-bit PWM cases; offset and gain plus INL budget (18.0 / 10.09 LSB).

**Monte Carlo claims.** I reproduced all the R-2R Monte Carlo claims with an independent nodal simulation of the voltage-mode ladder:

- 8-bit at 1%: about 12% of builds non-monotonic, worst DNL about 2 LSB.
- 8-bit at 0.1%: worst DNL about 0.2 LSB.
- 10-bit at 0.2%: 9% of builds non-monotonic.
- 12-bit at 0.1%: 29% of builds non-monotonic.

**Sims.** All sim params (adc-lab, sampling-aliasing, sample-hold, current-sense, dac-lab) are in the catalog.

**Figures.** I rendered all 8 circuit-bearing pages with preview-circuit and screenshotted the trickier plots (q0808 error sawtooth, q0816 C_H constraints, q0821 step response).
