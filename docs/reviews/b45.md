# QA review: batch b45 (q0789-q0807)

I reviewed all 19 pages: timers (q0789-q0793), interrupts (q0794-q0803) and peripheral interface drawings (q0804-q0807). I recomputed every quiz answer and worked number with `node`. Every quiz answer index, numeric value and tolerance was correct. I rendered every `circuit` block with preview-circuit (q0792, q0804 x3, q0805 x2, q0806 x2, q0807 x3) and checked each against the electrical rules: diode and body-diode directions, PMOS/NMOS pinout, junction dots and the opto pinout. All of them are electrically correct. I also checked every timing diagram cycle by cycle, and screenshotted the two I edited. Every sim id and parameter matches src/sims/catalog.json. Where a sim caption names a feature (the h-bridge-lab dead-time view, interrupt-lab missed/pending/latency, the gpio-lab debounce methods, timer-lab PSC/ARR), I confirmed it exists in the sim source.

**Numbers checked (sample).**

- **Timers:** f = fclk/((PSC+1)(ARR+1)) examples; 16-bit overflow 4.096 ms; 32-bit at 72 MHz is 59.6 s; millis() wraps at 49.7 days.
- **Clock accuracy:** ppm to seconds per day (20 ppm = 1.728 s; 1% = 14.4 min); 11.6 ppm and 23 ppm logger budgets; the 5333/5334 fractional divider (62.5 ppm).
- **Waveform generation:** 38 kHz IR carrier (ARR+1 = 421, 0.013% error); the 440 Hz tone at 0.017 cents.
- **Measurement and protocols:** Modbus 3.5-character gap of 4.01 ms; period vs gate measurement errors; ultrasonic distances.
- **Interrupt cost:** UART interrupt load; 12/24/6-cycle Cortex-M entry, exit and tail-chain timings; 32/104-byte frames; nesting stack sums; worst-case latency tables.
- **Ring buffer and power:** ring-buffer sizing; coin-cell life of 4.7 years.
- **Analog front end and buses:** NTC divider (1.650/2.543/0.871/0.215 V, -36.7 mV/°C, 45 LSB/°C); self-heating; I2C Rp limits (0.97 kΩ, 3.54 kΩ, 11.8 kΩ).
- **Buttons:** RC debounce timing (12.04 ms, 0.31 V bounce, 0.40 ms press).
- **LEDs:** resistors (260/270 Ω, 25.1 Ω/3.08 W, 5.1 Ω/0.63 W); gamma 2.2 duties.
- **Motor drive:** stall, speed (2387 rpm), flyback energy (18 mJ), ripple (0.15 A), dead-time loss and opto CTR margins.
