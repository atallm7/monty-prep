## Remaining concerns
- The "knee = 0.35/t_r" convention is still used throughout q0529/q0530. It is now flagged once in q0530, but a learner may meet Johnson's 0.5/t_r definition elsewhere.
- q0589 uses 3 ppm/°C for a "silicon-dominated" BGA. Typical plastic BGAs have an effective CTE of about 6–12 ppm/°C, so the corner strain is an upper-end illustration (it is already framed as order-of-magnitude).
- In q0583, the Maxwell plot curve continues past φ = π/4. It is labelled "valid at low φ" and the caption explains it, but trimming x_max to 0.785 would be cleaner.
