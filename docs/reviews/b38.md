- **Square waves and spectra:** Fourier and Parseval power fractions; Gibbs; pulse-train line heights (25% and 10% duty); trapezoid sinc tables (q0675, q0678 tables match to ±0.1 dB); the 48/52 duty 2nd harmonic.

**Standard-interface numbers checked (q0679-q0684):**
- **LVDS:** 3.5 mA into 100 Ω gives 350 mV (247-454 mV range), about 1.2 V offset, receiver ±100 mV, input 0-2.4 V.
- **RS-485:** -7 to +12 V common mode, ±200 mV threshold, ≥1.5 V into 54 Ω.
- **CAN:** dominant 3.5/1.5 V, recessive 2.5 V, receiver thresholds 0.5/0.9 V, common mode about -2 to +7 V, 120 Ω.
- **USB:** 90 Ω, HS swing about 400 mV.

These all match the commonly cited specs and were already hedged with UNCERTAIN callouts. One error: PCIe was listed as 100 Ω (now about 85 Ω for board routing).

**Ott radiation estimate (q0683):** E ≈ 1.26e-6·f·L·I/r is the correct Ott common-mode formula. 5 µA on 1 m at 100 MHz and 3 m gives 2.1e-4 V/m (46.4 dBµV/m), which checks. I added:
- the FCC Class B 3 m limit it is compared with (150 µV/m ≈ 43.5 dBµV/m, 88-216 MHz);
- a hedge that a 1 m cable is already λ/3 at 100 MHz, so the short-antenna formula is at the edge of its validity.

**Most serious:**
- **Ground bounce scaling (q0674 sim caption, q0677 technical, deep and follow-up):** these said halving the rise time doubles the bounce. With a capacitive load the current C·dV/dt doubles too, so the open-loop L·di/dt rises about 4×. Real drivers fall between 2× and 4×. Corrected in four places.
- **q0679:** listed PCIe as a 100 Ω interface; corrected to about 85 Ω for board routing.
- **q0684:** said "800 mV differential peak-to-peak" for SATA; that is not SATA's level. Generalized to PCIe (early generations, minimum about 800 mVppd), with a note to check the revision.
