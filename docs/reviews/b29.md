## Most serious
- **q0512 quiz answer was wrong.** The true/false item said a 10 nF part cannot hold |Z| < 0.1 Ω to a higher frequency than 1 µF at the same 0.5 nH. The answer key said false, but the 10 nF does: it dips below 0.1 Ω between 57 and 88 MHz near its SRF.
  - Cause: the page claimed the upper edge "depends only on L". That is only an asymptote, and it fails when $Z_0=\sqrt{L/C}>Z_t$.
  - Fix: added an exact-band column to the table, rewrote the explanation, and replaced the quiz item with a correct 10 µF vs 1 µF comparison.
- **q0506 sim caption was wrong.** It said the 'many-same' preset gives "impedance drops by N everywhere, without any peak". The sim actually shows a 268 mΩ anti-resonance at 1.8 MHz between the bulk capacitor and the 100 nF bank. Caption rewritten.
- **q0507 plane capacitance was 10× too high.** It said 0.1–1 nF/cm² at 0.1 mm; the correct value is about 38 pF/cm².
