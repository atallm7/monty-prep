I screenshotted every figure and the one sim in the app, and ran `preview-circuit` on all three schematics. Sim params match `src/sims/catalog.json`. The validator reports 0 errors.

**Standards and semiconductor figures checked from memory:**
- **IPC-2221 conductor spacing (q0641):** the internal and external-uncoated columns and the step plot match the standard's B1/B2 pattern up to 500 V. The coated column at 30 V or less was wrong (0.1 mm); B4 is 0.05 mm, and I fixed it.
- **Node figures (q0646):** the ≈45-50 nm CPP and 20-30 nm MMP, the 19 B transistors in about 104 mm² (≈183 MTr/mm²), the 1.5-1.8× per-node density gain, the 193i and EUV Rayleigh numbers, and the 858 mm² and high-NA 429 mm² reticle fields are consistent with public data. They were already hedged as estimates.

**Most serious:**
- **q0645 follow-up:** a 1200 mm² die was given a Poisson yield of e^-12 ≈ 0.0006%. 1200 mm² is 12 cm², so λ = 1.2 and the yield is about 30%. A factor-of-ten unit error.
- **q0648 spoken answer:** it said the 12 K/W down path in parallel with 20 K/W up gives 12.3 K/W. The model's down path is 32 K/W (12 + 20), and 32 ∥ 20 = 12.3.
- **q0637/q0636 spoke current:** these used the IPC-2221 external constant only. Planes are usually inner layers, where the internal constant gives about 0.6 A per spoke, not 1.2 A. Both values are now given.
- **q0647:** "LPDDR4X-8533" does not exist (LPDDR4X tops out at 4266 MT/s). Corrected to LPDDR5X-8533.
