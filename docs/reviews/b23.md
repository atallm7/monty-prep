# QA review: batch b23 (q0383–q0401)

I recomputed every worked example and quiz answer with `node`. That covered servo steady-state error, timer arithmetic, joint torque, motion-profile peak and RMS torque, stall heating, the NTC divider, the thermal I²t model, the ageing model, the emissivity error, the coil ampere-turn table and the J²ρ heating rates. I screenshotted every figure and sim in the running app (one shot per figure) and re-shot each figure I edited. I checked sim params against catalog.json and checked each caption against the sim source (servo-lab, dc-motor-lab, timer-lab, encoder-lab, flyback-protection, current-sense, rc-transient). I re-rendered every edited schematic with preview-circuit. The validator reports 0 errors.

**Most serious:**
- **Wrong efficiency claims in the dc-motor-lab captions (q0386, q0392).** The sim shows peak efficiency at about 5% of stall torque, close to no-load. The captions said it peaks "at mid speed and torque" (q0386) or "at a moderate load" (q0392).
- **q0392:** the loss breakdown contradicted its own motor model. It claimed 4.8 W of copper loss at R = 1.2 Ω, which is inconsistent with the stated output. I replaced it with a self-consistent split (Kt = 0.025 gives 8.3 W copper and 3.1 W friction, totalling 11.4 W).
- **q0398:** the emissivity formula and plot were described as an "emission-only model that ignores reflection". In fact the formula is the full grey-body model with the reflected background. The x-axis also ran to an unphysical ε = 1.3.
- **q0388:** the trapezoid-versus-triangle profile claim was backwards. A trapezoid needs more peak acceleration and less peak speed, not the other way round.
- **q0385:** the "orange 2× margin line" in the caption sat off the plot, because the y-axis stopped at 2.2 N·m.
