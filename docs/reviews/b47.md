# QA review: batch b47 (q0823-q0840)

I reviewed 18 pages: 8 on data storage and memory (q0823-q0830), 4 on debugging (q0831-q0834) and 6 on operating systems (q0835-q0840). I recomputed every quiz answer and worked number with `node`. All quiz answer indices, numeric answers and tolerances were correct. One quiz *explanation* drew a wrong conclusion (q0824).

**Numbers checked.**

- **Memory:** memory budgets and address bits; logger data rates and capacities (2.04 kB/s gives 7.34 MB/h and 176 MB/day; 10.4 GB in 30 days); SPI/QSPI bandwidth; PSRAM single-SPI access time (1.6 µs); NOR wear lifetimes (25 years and 2.3 days).
- **Persistence:** EEPROM and flash wear tables (11.6 days, 8.1 years, 2.7/21.9 years, 31.7 years); holdup energy (49.5 µJ needed, 180 µJ and 85.5 µJ available); CR2032 lifetime.
- **NV physics:** floating-gate electron count (18,700); Arrhenius factor 55 to 85 °C (26.1); the read-window margins.
- **ROM:** capacities; bit-line RC (2.05 ns); the sine-table sizes.
- **EEPROM:** I2C page and byte write times (1.51 ms, 326 ms); SPI vs I2C read times (26 ms vs 737 ms); the 0x3C/0x3E wrap cases; the 100 ms logging case (3.15e9 writes, about 200 KB).
- **Registers:** all register decodes and reassemblies (0xB5A3, 0xA3B5, 0xA634, 0x5C, 0x0F9C, 0x86, BSRR 0x0020_0000) and the `sext` code.
- **Debugging:** droop examples; printf timings; the SWD word rate (46 clocks per word gives 87k words/s and 0.75 s for 256 KB); crystal load caps (10 pF, 17 pF); the UART fallback error.
- **Operating systems:** tick overhead; RAM totals (3048 B); RMS bounds (n = 2, 3, 4 and ∞); all response-time iterations (R = 1/3/7; the 4th task R = 15; the follow-up R = 2/5/11); latency sums; power budget (2.67 µA, 9.4 years).

**Figures.** These pages have no circuit blocks, so preview-circuit was not applicable. I decoded the ROM diode-array dots row by row (matches the caption) and checked both RTOS timing diagrams slot by slot against the response-time analysis and the priority-inversion text. I screenshotted the q0827 ROM SVG, the q0840 timing diagrams, the q0839 latency plot and the q0826 Arrhenius plot. All sim ids and params exist in `src/sims/catalog.json`. I checked the register-decoder `ctrl` preset in `src/sims/models/register-decoder.ts`: it is a 32-bit timer register, not the page's CTRL map, so the q0830 caption was reworded.
