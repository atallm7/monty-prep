## Remaining concerns

- Several illustrative inputs are assumptions flagged UNCERTAIN on the pages: activation energies, electrolyte mass, thermal resistances, protection thresholds and delays, and LFP hysteresis. They are reasonable, but they are not data.
- In q0329 the nail-short melt time is order-of-magnitude only. Contact resistance at the nail, which the model ignores, probably dominates in real cells.
