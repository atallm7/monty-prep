# Deepen pass review: e02 (op-amps, GBW, slew, rise time, PLL, PDN, matching)

Scope: the `::: deep` sections added in the deepen pass (diff vs 65d169b) for the 19 ids in docs/deepen/e02.txt, plus the q0118 follow-up the writer changed. I recomputed every new number with node. No new figures were added, so there was nothing to render. `validate-content` reports 0 errors and no warnings for these ids.

- **q0116**: Loop-gain paragraph: with T at -90°, |T|=10 gives about 0.5% magnitude error (1/(2|T|²)) and 5.7° of phase error. The text had said "gain error near 1/|T|", which is now fixed. The NG=A0 limit now says the bandwidth is 2·f_p, not f_p. The follower PM at f2=fT now gives the exact value of about 52° (45° by the simple estimate) and 16% overshoot (ζ=0.5). Bench corner: NG = 1+99k/1k = 100 exactly, so the corner is 10 kHz, not "10^6/101 = 9.9 kHz".
- **q0117**: OK. The SR/GBW sizing (50 µA, 7.96 MHz, ratio = V_ov = 0.2 V), the settling budget (18.4 + 6.96 = 25.4 µs vs 10.98 µs, ×2.3) and the 20-80% bench numbers all check out.
- **q0118**: The SPI example's "40 ns clean window per half-cycle" is now about 20 ns per 25 ns half-cycle. The 200 MHz scope reading is now 5.4 ns, not 5.3 ns. Rewrote the edge-shape bullet: it claimed a linear ramp has more high-harmonic content than an RC edge for the same 10-90% time. That is backwards: both roll off at 40 dB/dec and the ramp's envelope is 0.25 vs 0.35 ×1/(f·t_r); truly smooth edges are what roll off faster. **420 MHz follow-up**: the ~8 dB is confirmed (1/(π·420 MHz·2 ns) = 0.379, -8.4 dB). I fixed two things there: 420 MHz is on the 20 dB/dec segment, not the "flat part"; and the parenthetical now says the benefit grows only up to the old 640 MHz knee, then levels off at 20·log10(4) = 12 dB.
- **q0121**: The "Aβ→1" limit wrongly called the correction "enormous" with tens-of-percent error. It now says this happens at the closed-loop bandwidth β·GBW, where the gain is -3 dB and the phase is 45°. Bench: at 1 kHz, a 1 MHz GBW part has |A| ≈ 1000, so the input difference is about 1 mVpp, not 10 µV (that used A0).
- **q0122**: Minor change only: the open-loop output now "saturates near +12 V" instead of "sits at +12 V". The rest is OK (5Vp − 4Vn, difference-amp divider).
- **q0123**: OK. Half-cycle charge is 133 nC, sag is 1.33 V / 13 mV, mid-rail τ is 50 ms with a 3.2 Hz corner.
- **q0124**: The Z_out example used β = 0.1. This inverting gain-10 stage has β = 1/11, so the values are now 8.2 mΩ / 41 µV at DC and about 8 Ω / 41 mV at 10 kHz. Bench Rule 3: the DMM reading is dominated by the 1 mV offset (plus 50 µV), not "tens of microvolts".
- **q0126**: Reworded the LDO analogy. The bench caveat now says 0.1% resistors give a 10-20% uncertainty in A0 rather than "swamped". The V_A/2V_T derivation, 967² = 119 dB and the temperature factor 0.69 (-3.2 dB) all check out.
- **q0127**: The threshold-skew explanation was wrong. 1% resistors change the 100 mV window by about 1-2 mV, while a 1% V_ref error shifts the whole window by 25 mV. The divider current is now "at most" about 10 µA. The threshold maths (2.255/2.745 V; 470 kΩ → 104 mV, 2.448/2.552 V) is OK.
- **q0130**: The derated corner is about 1250 Hz (25%), not 1240 Hz (24%). The 12-bit ADC LSB is about -72 dB, not -74 dB.
- **q0131**: The two-pole text said "each pole contributes 1/(1+x²)". That is the pair together; each pole is 1/√(1+x²). The 139 Hz, 10.6 ms (9.23τ) and ×54 numbers are correct.
- **q0132**: The brick-wall bullet said "unbounded" step response. It is now bounded but non-causal, with 9% Gibbs overshoot and a product of about 0.45 (computed). Fixed the bench diagnosis: a second pole with overshoot doesn't make the time-domain BW "lower". Clarified the cross-reference to the 5 ns/3 ns follow-up.
- **q0133**: The 0.1 dB sensitivity is about 5%, not 6%. The rest (3.55/3.52 kHz, phases, XY ellipse sin φ) is OK.
- **q0134**: The PFD-rate argument now correctly says output in-band noise scales with f_out/f_PFD (23.5 vs 41.6 dB at 120 MHz), so the 18 dB saving holds. A 240 vs 480 MHz VCO choice doesn't change it. This also fixes the "choose lowest N" contradiction, since the example picked 480 MHz. Removed the odd "price is a faster loop filter" phrasing.
- **q0135**: Rewrote the cold-start acquisition paragraph. A tri-state PFD with a frequency error gives UP about 50% of the time on average (checked by simulation), not "nearly all the time". C1 must reach the full 1 V in lock, since the IR term vanishes. Acquisition is therefore about 40 µs, and lock about 40-60 µs, not 20-40 µs. The N=100 fix now says that raising I_cp tenfold restores both f_n and ζ, while raising R alone restores only ζ.
- **q0136**: Replaced a meaningless charge-pump bullet with one on output-resistance-dependent mismatch. Corrected the C2 bullet: reducing C2 raises its pole and recovers phase margin, but filters less ripple. It does not "widen the bandwidth". The spur estimate (2 fC, 20 ps, 10 µV, -100/-112 dBc) is OK as an order of magnitude.
- **q0137**: A large N does not make "each division step tiny", because the integer-N step is f_PFD. That is fixed. Bench: letting the VCO drift doesn't confirm the tuning sign, so the text now says to force V_ctrl. The divider plan (R=4, N=100, M=4) and the filter values (1.52 nF, 1.47 kΩ, 76 pF, 1.43 MHz) are OK.
- **q0140**: OK. The C = I·t/ΔV table, 90 ps, 556 ns / 28 ns ramps and 5.4 mV IR all check out.
- **q0141**: Added a safety warning: the half-voltage method applied to a Li-ion cell means about 10 A, near a short. The method is now limited to low-power sources. Softened "0.1-0.2 Ω for single cells", because large cells are tens of mΩ. The L-network maths (Q = 1.732, 34.5 nH, 55.1 pF, 0.48 W with Q_L = 50) is OK.

Remaining concerns: the q0136 spur estimate treats the cycle's charge pulse as a sinusoidal ripple. It is flagged as an order of magnitude with an UNCERTAIN callout, which is acceptable. The q0117 "slew-induced overshoot" bullet is plausible but depends on topology; I left it as written.
