- **Timing diagrams:** I decoded every I²C timing diagram bit by bit (q0737, q0744, q0745, q0748, q0749, q0751, q0752). Address and data bits match the captions, and SDA changes only while SCL is low except at START and STOP.

**Thresholds verified (q0739-q0744).**

- I²C: V_IL = 0.3 V_DD and V_IH = 0.7 V_DD; V_OL ≤ 0.4 V at 3 mA (Standard/Fast); t_r limits 1000/300/120 ns; C_b 400 pF (550 pF Fm+); Fast-mode t_LOW/t_HIGH 1.3/0.6 µs and t_BUF 1.3 µs; t_SU;DAT 250/100 ns.
- TTL/LVTTL: 0.8/2.0 V in, 0.4/2.4 V out.
- CMOS: 0.3/0.7 V_DD.
- 1.8 V and 1.2 V LVCMOS: 0.35/0.65 V_DD; 1.8 V outputs V_DD − 0.45 V and 0.45 V.
- RS-485 ±200 mV, LVDS 350 mV at 1.2 V common mode, CAN dominant ≈2 V differential.
- **SMBus:** the page stated "a 100 kHz maximum" and thresholds "about 0.8 / 2.1 V" as general facts. These are SMBus 2.0 values. SMBus 3.0 (2014) added 400 kHz and 1 MHz classes and lowered V_IH to 1.35 V, and SMBus always has a 10 kHz *minimum* clock. q0744 is corrected in the family-tree bullet, the followup and the quiz option wording.

**TI SLVA689 citation (q0739).** SLVA689 is TI's application report on I²C pull-up sizing. I'm confident of the number and topic, so I kept the citation and corrected the title to its published form, "I2C Bus Pullup Resistor Calculation".

**Figures and sims.** I screenshotted every figure and sim one at a time and rendered all circuits with preview-circuit (q0737, q0739, q0743, q0746, q0750). The level-shifter FET pinout is correct: drain on the high side, body diode from source to drain.
