## Remaining concerns
- q0369: the 2×10⁻⁵ kg·m² rotor-plus-prop inertia (138 J) is on the high side for a small drone motor. It is labelled as an assumption.
- q0481: the 6 mm stub row only applies to thick backplanes. A 1.6 mm board cannot have a stub that long.
- The q0288 sim's internal R_out uses the RSS form (8.25 Ω), while its deep section quotes the exact coth value (8.17 Ω). The page explains the difference, but the student sees both numbers.
