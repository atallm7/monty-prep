# QA review: batch b20 (q0325-q0344)

I recomputed every worked example and quiz answer with `node`. That covered:
- the charger and short-circuit currents, I²R and heating rates, internal-short power splits, Arrhenius factors and the Semenov crossings;
- the solenoid force and inductance, the winding-window table, the motor second-order start-up peaks, plugging, cold-copper and AC-impedance numbers;
- the flyback decay times.

I screenshotted every figure in the running app, one element at a time, and read each one. For every embedded sim I ran its model with the page's parameters (lipo-lab, cell-balancing, dc-motor-lab, inductor-saturation, rc-transient, flyback-protection, pwm-lab) and checked that the caption matches what the sim actually shows. The validator reports 0 errors.

**Most serious:**
- Several sim captions described behaviour the sims do not show:
  - q0331 passed `crate: 10` to lipo-lab, which clamps it to 5C.
  - q0327 started lipo-lab at a setting where the cell cuts off almost at once (0.06% delivered).
  - q0335's inductor-saturation pulse never reached Isat, and the sim's air core keeps the same L0, so the caption's "lower inductance" was wrong.
  - q0339's caption invited changing an inductance that pwm-lab has no control for.
  - q0332 claimed the total-heat readout scales as I², but it scales roughly as I.
- q0340 contained wrong flyback decay-rate statements: "70 A/s, nearly linear" for the diode clamp, and "35 times faster" for the Zener in the quiz explanation. The real figures are about 1270 A/s, exponential, and about 7 times faster.
- q0334's two SVGs were physically wrong: the solenoid gap was drawn on the spring side, and the motor loop forces were drawn on conductors parallel to B.
- The q0327 schematic had a floating NTC.
