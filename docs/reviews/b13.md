- **q0201**: The applications section credited the "TPS62xxx/TPS54xxx" families with an internal bootstrap capacitor. That is inaccurate: many parts use an external BOOT capacitor, and some members of those families are PFET or asynchronous designs. I generalized it to a part class. I rewrote the meaningless "automotive ... often hybrid parts" bullet. The plot's unitless axis was rendering "900 m", so I removed `"unit":""`.
- **q0202**: Physics error. The page said a *low-side* FET's drain-body avalanche clamps a negative SW. A negative SW actually forward-biases that body diode. The real clamp with no freewheel path is the *high-side* FET avalanching at SW ≈ V_in − BV_DSS. I fixed the technical and deep sections. I added the correct energy: about 19 µJ, or about 9 W, when Q1 avalanches, versus 13 µJ, or 6.7 W, for a clamp from SW to ground. The quiz now says "a clamp to ground". Other fixes:
  - The Schottky-vs-silicon saving was 0.25 W; it is now 0.5 W, since (1−D)·2 A·0.35 V = 0.51 W.
  - "20 to 100 V/ns" is now "10 V/ns-class" (computed: 12.5 V/ns).
  - Moved the SW label, which overlapped the C_in label.
- **q0203**: In the table, the sync loss at 5 V out was 0.023 W; it is now 0.024 W.
- **q0204**: Added the missing junction dot where the level-shifter source meets ground. "Level-shifter shoot-through" is now "static current". Fixed the unitless plot axis.

I also corrected the boot-resistor description. A resistor in series with the bootstrap *diode* only limits recharge current. Turn-on slows only if the resistor sits between the BST pin and C_boot.
