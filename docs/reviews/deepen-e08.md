- **q0478**: (1) The return-current crossover explanation said it "counts only the signal trace's own resistance". (2) The layer-change worked example put a *ground* stitching via in an S/G/P/S ground-to-power transition, where no via can join the planes. - **q0486**: Two physics errors in the TIA example. (1) It said the feedback resistor's capacitance to the plane "adds in parallel with Rf".
