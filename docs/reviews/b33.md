# QA review: batch b33 (q0574-q0592)

I recomputed every worked example and quiz answer with `node`. That covered via R/L/C, the IPC-2221 via-current fit (0.84 A per 0.3 mm via at 10 °C internal; about 570 °C predicted for 5 A), thermal resistances, stub resonances and the S21 notch, aspect ratios, solder-wicking volumes, the CTE strain, loop inductance, reflection vs rise time, sheet resistance and necks, λ/20, probe impedances, the leakage divider and the buck input current. All quiz answers and tolerances are correct.

I screenshotted every figure and sim in the running app (44 in all) and read each one. I re-shot every figure I edited, and I previewed the q0589 schematic after fixing it. Both sims (scope-probe `loading` and dmm-lab `4wire`) use valid catalog params. I rewrote both sim captions to match the sim defaults. The validator reports 0 errors.

**Claims checked:**
- **IPC-4761 type names** (I tented, II tented and covered, III plugged, IV plugged and covered, V filled, VI filled and covered, VII filled and capped) match the standard as it is commonly cited. I removed the "my recollection" wording in q0579 and added the a/b (one-/two-sided) variants as a hedged note.
- **IPC-6012 plating** (about 20 µm average for Class 2 and 25 µm for Class 3 through holes) is right, and it is already hedged on every page that uses it.
- **Via-current estimate:** the internal-layer IPC-2221 fit applied to the barrel cross-section (33.5 mil²) gives 0.84 A at 10 °C, as stated. Each page already calls it conservative and indicative, and points to IPC-2152.

**Most serious errors:**
- **q0583, q0584, q0585 (the "10 mm × 0.1 mm neck" model):** these pages treated the web between two antipads as a 10 mm-long, 0.1 mm-wide conductor carrying the full 3 A, which gave 49 mΩ and 150 mV. A real row of vias gives many short webs in parallel. The examples are now framed as a worst case where current is forced along a single channel, with the correct physical picture added.
- **q0585 stagger math:** the page claimed that staggering raises the web from 0.1 mm to 0.22 mm. That is wrong, because the in-row pitch is unchanged. At the same via density, even a hexagonal layout only reaches about 0.17 mm. The page now says the benefit of staggering is that no straight slot forms.
- **q0584 thermal reliefs:** the page said spoke resistance is "far above the barrel". By the page's own numbers it is 0.15 mΩ, against 1.3 mΩ for the barrel. The real issues are ampacity and inductance, and vias don't need reliefs at all.
- **q0577 stack-up:** the SVG and process flow showed L2/L5 as foils laminated in the same press as the outer layers. That is physically impossible, because those inner layers could never be etched. Both now show L1-L2 and L5-L6 cores. The page also now says that a single-core buried via adds a drill/plate cycle rather than an extra press.
- **q0575 SVG:** the ground via was drawn straight through the L4 signal trace, which would short it. It is now drawn offset beside the trace, and the caption explains this. The answer also said a 0 Ω resistor connects layers, which contradicted the technical section; I fixed it.
- **q0590 diode test:** the page claimed the reverse diode-mode reading is "open". In practice it goes through the upper clamp into the unpowered rail, so it reads higher or drifts. I fixed this in three places.
