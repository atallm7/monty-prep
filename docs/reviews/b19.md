# QA review: batch b19 (q0305-q0324)

I recomputed the quiz answers, tables and worked examples with `node`. Every page that quotes "model" numbers was checked against the simulator's own code: I ran `src/sims/models/lipo-lab.ts`, `soc-estimator.ts`, `cell-balancing.ts` and `regulator-compare.ts` directly with the embedded parameters.

For figures, I rendered every schematic with `preview-circuit.mjs` and screenshotted every figure and every embedded sim (one element at a time) in the running app. All quiz answers were correct. The validator reports 0 errors.

The same fix appears on several pages: the batch claimed that copper current-collector dissolution starts at about 2.5 V cell voltage. It actually needs a deep over-discharge (anode about 3.3 V vs Li/Li+, cell well below 2 V). 2.5 V is a manufacturer floor with margin. I reworded this on q0305, q0309, q0311, q0314, q0316 and q0324.
